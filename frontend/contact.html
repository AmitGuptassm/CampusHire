FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I apply for a job?

Create a student account, complete your profile, browse available opportunities and apply for jobs for which you are eligible.

Can recruiters post jobs?

Yes. Recruiters can create a recruiter account and, once approved by the placement administrator, post opportunities for students.

How can I track my application?

After logging in, students can view their applications and track their current status from the My Applications section.

How do I contact the placement team?

You can use the contact form above or reach the placement team through the email and phone details provided on this page.
